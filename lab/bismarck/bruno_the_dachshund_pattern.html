Bruno the Dachshund

A sausage-dog amigurumi with a striped blue-and-white body, floppy ears, a beige snout and a stubby tail. Transcribed from the Xuxu Crochet video tutorial — every row links to the moment it appears on screen. Tick rows off as you crochet; your progress is saved.

Materials

  • Brown #26 milk-cotton 4-ply — body, head, legs, ears, tail
  • White #63 — body stripes
  • Blue #54 — body stripes
  • Dark beige #18 — snout
  • Black #21 — embroidered mouth
  • 2.5 mm crochet hook, 4 mm safety eyes, stuffing, tapestry needle

Gauge isn’t critical, but crochet tightly enough that stuffing can’t peek through. This pattern is worked in joined, turned rows, not a continuous spiral: nearly every row starts “slst, 1ch”, which keeps each tube flat and gives a tidy edge for sewing.

Reading a row: the count in [brackets] is the total stitches at the end of the row. “(sc, inc)*3” means repeat “sc in the next stitch, then 2 sc in the one after” three times — starting from 6, you finish with 9.

To change colour: work the last stitch until two loops remain on the hook, drop the old colour, pull the new one through both loops. Changes always land on a row boundary, so stripes stay crisp. Finish: slst, cut, leave a long tail.

Assembly

Sew with the long tails you saved, using small whip stitches through back loops so seams stay invisible. Head first — leg placement depends on it.

  1. Sew the head to the body. Centre it on the brown tip and go around twice — this joint carries the most weight. 31:30
  2. Sew the legs: front pair just behind the head, back pair near the tail, evenly spaced so Bruno stands level. 34:12
  3. Sew the nose onto the beige snout, point facing down and outward. 37:30
  4. Sew the ears to the top sides of the skull, flopping forward over the stripes. 39:22
  5. Sew the tail to the back tip, angled slightly upward. 40:38
  6. Glue in the 4 mm eyes above the nose, symmetric about the snout. For a baby or pet, embroider them with black yarn instead. 42:04
  7. Dab fabric glue at the base of each ear so the curl holds. 42:38
  8. Embroider the mouth with black yarn: a short line from the nose tip splitting into a small smile. 43:14

Weave in the ends, give Bruno a final shaping squish — done.
